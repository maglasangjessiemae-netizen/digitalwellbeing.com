Using Technology Wisely, Safely, and Responsibly

What is Digital Well-being?

Digital well-being means using technology in a healthy, balanced, and responsible way. It involves understanding how our digital habits affect our physical health, emotions, relationships, learning, and everyday life.

Technology can help us learn, communicate, create, and accomplish many things. However, using technology without balance can affect our focus, sleep, relationships, and overall well-being.

"Technology should improve our lives, not control them."

Healthy Screen Time

Screens are useful for studying, communicating, entertainment, and creativity. The important thing is to use them intentionally and make time for activities away from screens.

Take Breaks

Take regular breaks when using computers, phones, or other digital devices for long periods.

Get Enough Sleep

Avoid using devices when it is time to sleep and give yourself time to rest.

Stay Active

Balance screen activities with physical activities, hobbies, and time with family and friends.

Online Safety

Being responsible online also means protecting yourself and respecting other people. We should think carefully before sharing information or interacting with people online.

Remember: Never share sensitive personal information with strangers online. Be careful with suspicious links, messages, and websites.

Important Online Safety Practices

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Keep personal information private.
  • Check information before sharing it.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Use privacy and security settings.
  • Report harmful or inappropriate online behavior.
  • Ask a trusted adult for help when something online feels unsafe.

UNICEF Philippines also recommends being aware of online risks such as cyberbullying, scams, misinformation, and other harmful content.

Responsible Social Media Use

Social media allows people to communicate, share ideas, and express themselves. However, responsible users should think about the possible effects of their posts and comments.

Think Before You Post

Ask yourself if your post is true, respectful, appropriate, and necessary.

Respect Others

Avoid insulting, embarrassing, or spreading harmful information about other people.

Check Information

Do not immediately believe or share information. Check reliable sources first.

Effects of Excessive Technology Use

Technology can be beneficial, but too much or poorly balanced use can interfere with important parts of everyday life.

  • Difficulty concentrating on schoolwork
  • Less time for physical activities
  • Interrupted sleep or poor sleep habits
  • Less face-to-face interaction
  • Distraction from important responsibilities
  • Exposure to harmful or misleading online content

Tips for Better Digital Well-being

Try These Habits:

  • Set reasonable limits for recreational screen use.
  • Take breaks from your devices.
  • Keep your phone away while studying.
  • Have screen-free time with family and friends.
  • Choose useful and educational content.
  • Protect your privacy and personal information.
  • Think before posting or commenting.
  • Balance online activities with sleep, exercise, hobbies, and real-life relationships.

UNICEF's digital responsibility materials emphasize being conscious of digital habits, setting time limits, taking breaks, and using technology intentionally.

Our Call to Action

Use Technology Wisely.
Stay Safe. Stay Balanced. Stay Responsible.

Technology is a powerful tool. Instead of allowing technology to control our time and attention, we can choose how we use it. Small responsible habits can help us create a healthier and safer digital life.
